## About UNICHAT

**UNICHAT** is a student-built communication platform designed specifically for the **Mindanao State University – Iligan Institute of Technology (MSU-IIT)** community. 

It provides students with a secure, real-time environment to connect with peers, exchange ideas, and participate in campus conversations while preserving individual privacy.

---

## Key Platform Features

* **Verified Student Network:** Access is exclusively available to verified university accounts (`@g.msuiit.edu.ph`), ensuring an authentic campus community while preventing unauthorized access and spam.
* **Privacy-First Matching:** Students can engage in one-to-one anonymous text conversations. Personal student details are never revealed to chat partners.
* **Ephemeral Conversations:** Messages exist only for the duration of the active session with a strict five-minute rolling retention window. Once conversations end, messages are permanently purged.
* **Campus Freedom Wall:** A real-time community bulletin board where students can share thoughts, discuss university topics, listen to embedded song recommendations, and interact through comment threads.
* **Seasonal Campus Themes:** Dynamic, event-driven interface makeovers throughout the academic year—including Halloween, Christmas, and Classic MSU-IIT maroon-and-gold editions.
* **Safety and Moderation:** Comprehensive community guidelines supported by user reporting workflows, automated word masking, and administrative moderation tools to maintain a safe campus space.
